Attack sheet

All 36 attacks of the Rivals 2 Mollo build, as they go into the Unreal ATT_ assets: windows, hitboxes and on-hit properties. Frame data from Rivals Academy (RoA1 patch 2.1.5.6), 60 fps.

Hitbox offsets are in centimeters, in Blender bone space (Y runs along the bone). Knockback is RoA1's as-is and still needs balancing in R2. Mollo had no grab in RoA1, so grabs and throws are new designs.

Specials

NSpecial

18 frames · AN_Mol_NSpecial · Both · props: Bomb
Startup4Pull12Recovery2

Design notes (FR) Sort une bombe de la ceinture. Direction tenue : haut = Cerise, gauche = Petard, droite = Flashbang, bas = Finisher, neutre = Cerise. Meche de 120 images des f16, puis instable et explose 50 images plus tard ; explose aussi 50 images apres le lancer, au contact, ou si Mollo est touche. Implementation R2 : article tenable (Is Holdable Item) monte sur la socket Item ; les lancers passent par les animations ItemThrow*.
